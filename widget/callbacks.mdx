---
title: 'Callbacks'
description: 'Respond to widget actions with callback functions'
---

The widget calls the functions you pass in `on` when something happens (it is ready, a search finishes, a location is selected, filters change, an error occurs). They only tell you what happened; they cannot change how the widget behaves.

See [Events](/widget/events) for every callback, its arguments and a working example.
